6. "My phone's screen-time report arrives every Sunday like a disappointed parent, then offers five more minutes of the app it just shamed me for." — Discarded: decent, but screen-time shaming is a familiar joke and it roasts an app feature rather than a gadget's behaviour; also the longest of the candidates.
